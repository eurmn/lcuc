# ARAM MAYHEM RULES & MECHANICS

## Overview
- Mode: Featured queue based on ARAM (Howling Abyss, Butcher's Bridge, Koeshin's Crossing).
- Loadout: Runes disabled entirely. Keystone runes are only accessible as augments. Exhaust disabled.
- Spells exclusive: A Dance with Death, Brass Blast, Get Jinxed!, Growth Spurt, Poltergeist.

## Augment Structure
- Breakpoints: Levels 3 (spawn), 7, 11, 15. Total 4 base slots. 5th slot reserved for Quest reward augments.
- Screen: 3 augment choices per tier. 1 reroll per offering (6 possible per round).
- Tiers: Silver, Gold, Prismatic. Same tier offered to all players simultaneously.
- First two rounds cannot both be Silver. Augments offered once per game.

## Global Stat Alterations
- Overcap Critical Strike: Every 1% Crit over 100% converts to +0.45 bonus AD or +0.75 AP (Adaptive).
- Attack Speed Cap: Raised to 5.0 (from 2.5).
- Combo Breaker: 5 out of last 7 seconds CC'd -> instant cleanse + 3s CC immunity. Stacking tenacity outside concurrent chains.
- Innate Runes: All champions possess Presence of Mind permanently.
- Base Stats: Bonus HP (+20-600), mana/energy regen +10%, bonus resistances (+15-25 armor/MR for melee).
- Relics: 22% missing HP and 22% missing mana.

## Headliners
- Random event roll at match start.
- Command Line: Hacked rerolls upgrade offering tier (Silver -> Gold, Gold -> Prismatic).
- Cafe Cuties: Health relics gain 20% missing HP beam + base relics between Nexus turrets.
- HEARTSTEEL: Augment cards with stickers give bonus: Aphelios (2 Stat Anvils), Ezreal (AD+AP), Kayn (5% Omnivamp), K'Sante (+1 Level cap 19), Sett (bonus HP), Yone (AS + AH).
- K/DA: Collect photocards dropped every 12s; complete all 4 -> bonus augment selection on death.
- PENTAKILL: Exclusive augment screen for Q/W/E haste+damage boost; at level 18 grant DOOM (Ult boost).
- SPACE GROOVE: Center dance floor disco ball charges up -> 100g every 0.5s up to 1000g for nearby champions.
- THE BEE BAND: Bees/honey fly across lane; bees deal 10-20% max HP true damage, honey heals 15-30% and grants gold.
- TRUE DAMAGE: Kills and turrets drop Stat Anvils of random tier.


## Recommender model (v3, overlay)
- Effects estimated in log-odds, Normal-Normal empirical Bayes shrink (tau^2 via DerSimonian-Laird, floor 0.01).
- Build prediction: L = logit(p_champ) + sum(beta) + sum(0.70^k * gamma), tanh-saturated at 1.5, 80% band.
- Hierarchy for a champion x resource cell is champion baseline -> that resource's own global ARAM
  effect -> champion x resource counts -> personal counts. The level-2 prior is the resource's
  global effect (`p_glob_resource`), NOT the champion baseline: a cell with 1 game must fall back
  to "how strong is this item in ARAM", not to "how strong is this champion".
- Item ranking: `(wpa + sum of pair LCBs) * 3000/gold`. Pair synergy is scored on its Wilson lower
  confidence bound, so a +10% point estimate from a handful of games cannot float an item to the
  top; the point estimate is still what the row reports as synergy. Missing gold cost counts as
  3000g (the catalogue median) so unknown items get no cost-efficiency bonus.
- The index ingests every `aram_participants` row (600k on a mature install, ~50 s) and caches the
  compiled counts to `cache/mayhem_synergy_index.bin`, keyed by the databases' size+mtime: later
  starts load in <1 s and any new rows trigger one background rebuild.
- Reroll EV: exact expected max of a 3-card SRSWOR draw from the unseen tier pool; P(improve) = 1 - C(m,3)/C(P,3).
- Overlay renders natively (DIB + GDI text, right-edge row stack). No HTML over the game.
- Card rows are labelled LEFT/MID/RIGHT (screen order) and PICK/REROLL/SKIP (engine verdict).
- Build panel: three stacked rows on the right edge, above the minimap. Row 1 is the three
  next items, each with its own rank, WR/lift/sample line and the engine's reason
  ("Pairs with X (+2.1% synergy)" or the standalone lift). Row 2 is the full build in
  purchase order (boots included). Row 3 is up to six alternatives with their lift, i.e. the
  candidates that scored well but lost the ranking. Items are ranked by
  `(wpa + synergy_lift) * 3000/gold`, with wpa from empirical-Bayes-shrunk win rates, so the
  order is the model's, not pick-rate popularity.
- The panel recomputes on every augment pick, on every inventory change (the live-game
  poller fingerprints the sorted item ids) and at match start, so both new augments and
  bought items re-rank the remaining slots.
- Hotkeys: F8 rescan · F9/F10/F11 record the pick on LEFT/MID/RIGHT · F6 undo a pick ·
  F7 clear the cards.

## In-game augment flow
- The panel is detected automatically (`probe_panel`): three similar bright runs in one row
  with a dimmed backdrop above them. On detection the scan runs by itself, so the player
  never has to press F8 mid-fight; failures of those automatic scans are silent.
- Picks are remembered in `IN_GAME_AUGMENTS` for the whole game and cleared when the game
  ends. Every later scan uses them as `locked_augments`, and each pick immediately
  recomputes the item build (`recompute_in_game_build`) and the card verdicts.
- Pick detection has two paths: the F9/F10/F11 hotkeys (always reliable) and watching for a
  card that brightens **over its own first-probe brightness** just before the panel closes
  (`picked_from_baseline`). Comparing cards against each other does not work: the middle
  card is ~1.16x brighter on every panel regardless of art, so absolute brightness would
  call every middle card a pick. Two consecutive agreeing probes are required; anything
  ambiguous is left for the hotkeys, and the overlay toasts "Picked which card?" then.
- A reroll swaps the art without closing the panel, so the watcher compares a 4x4 luma grid
  per card (`signature_changed`, per-card NCC) and rescans when it changes.
- 26 of the 242 augments (`IsAbilityAugment`) share **three generic icons** — one per
  rarity: 6 silver (Siphon, Bolstered, It's Go Time, Stay Resolute, Trusty Weapon,
  Adaptive Ward), 14 gold, 6 prismatic. Art names only the class there, so the card is
  resolved from text: the title first, then the body text (`description_rect`, the card's
  inner width, OCR'd at 2x since body glyphs are ~11 px on a 720p capture) scored by
  distinctive-word overlap against each candidate's tooltip. If neither decides, the row
  shows `Name ?` / "unread card" instead of a confident wrong name. A fuzzy read may never
  escape a shared-art group: that is how Siphon was reported as "Spin Me Right Round".
- Augments are identified by their ARAM pool key (`aram_augment_id_for`), never by name
  hashing: "Jeweled Gauntlet" exists as Arena id 48 and ARAM id 1048, and only 1048 has
  statistics. `augment_numeric_id` is the single place that resolves names to ids.


## Reading the cards off screen (`services/augment_scan.rs`)
- Source of truth for what can be offered: `Maps/ModeSpecificData/Augments/*` (`__type == "AugmentData"`) in
  `raw.communitydragon.org/latest/game/maps/modespecificdata/kiwi.bin.json`, joined to `cherry-augments.json`
  and `lol.stringtable.json` for display names. `AugmentLargeIconPath` is the art the card actually draws.
- Identification is primary **card art**, not OCR: 32x32 masked NCC fingerprint against one thumbnail per
  distinct art (194 arts for 242 augments on 2026-11 patch). No OCR at all when the art maps to one augment.
- Riot reuses art across augments (~29% of the pool shares its art with another augment), so ties fall back
  to OCR of the title strip only (x = card, y = 42-55% of card height, 2x upscale), scored against the tied
  names only. A title matching nothing may overrule a bad art match.
- Card geometry comes from the bright bar along the cards' top edge: 3 bright runs in one row, centres from
  the runs, width = 0.866 * centre pitch, art = 0.50 * width at 0.12 * width below the bar.
- The whole scan is ~100-150 ms (release). Stats and verdicts are computed afterwards in a spawned task.
