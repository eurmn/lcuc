# ARAM MAYHEM ITEMS & EXCLUSIVES

## Mayhem-Exclusive Legendary Items
- Atma's Reckoning (Price: 3000g):
  - Stats: +700 Health, +30 Armor, +30 Magic Resist.
  - Passive (Intimidate): Gain bonus Attack Damage equal to 2% of bonus health.
  - Ideal for: Heavy tanks, juggernauts, Goliath augment users.
- Rite of Ruin (Price: 2800g):
  - Stats: +70 Ability Power, +400 Mana, +25 Ability Haste.
  - Passive: Casting spells grants shields to nearby allies and bursts damage.
  - Ideal for: Enchanter/battle mages, high AP casters.
- Sword of Blossoming Dawn (Price: 2700g):
  - Stats: +40 Ability Power, +20% Attack Speed, +200 Health, +15 Ability Haste.
  - Passive: Basic attacks heal lowest health nearby ally based on AP and attack speed.
  - Ideal for: Enchanters, hybrid on-hit, Marksmage enchanters (Kayle, Lulu, Milio).

## Augment Quest Reward Items
- Wooglet's Witchcap (AP Capstone): Huge AP boost + Stasis active (combines Rabadon's and Zhonya's).
- Golden Spatula (All-Rounder Capstone): Grants AD, AP, AS, Armor, MR, MS, and On-Hit procs.
- Ultra Hydra: Massive cleave damage scaling with maximum HP and AD.
- Void Immolation: Mega Sunfire aura burning nearby enemies with true damage.
- Jarvan I's: Grants extreme armor, MR, and aura durability.
